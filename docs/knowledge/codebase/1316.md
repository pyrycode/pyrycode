# Ticket #1316 — Probe instrument: prove the published record's lateness bound is the classified sighting's, measured against a first-poll miss (offline)

Joins the two tiers `lateness_bounded` sat between and neither one measured
end to end. The record tier (`TestFinTrailerRecordCarriesTheBoundAndItsDiscriminator`
and every other `finTrailerBuild` call site) pinned `Bounded` on
`trailBoundFromMiss` with the discriminator **handed in** as a literal or
table field — nothing there ever met a poll. The carrier tier (#1312's
`TestFinGatherSightingReportsTheMissBound`) measured the discriminator
against a real first-poll miss and deliberately stopped at the carrier. Since
#1320 the builder takes the gather's `finSighting`, so one poll drives both
the classified outcome and the published record — but that was a structural
argument from the input type, not a measurement. This ticket adds the row
that measures it: a record built from the composition's classified sighting,
beside a record built over the same bytes from a second observation, so the
two are separated by measurement rather than by the builder's input type.
Split from #1308; blockers #1320, #1312, #1315 and siblings #1321/#1324/#1325
all merged before this landed. No production code, no new symbol, no
signature change.

## Implementation

- **`TestFinGatherRecordPublishesTheMeasuredMissBound`**, placed immediately
  after `TestFinGatherSightingReportsTheMissBound` in
  `finding_run_gather_test.go` — the placement is load-bearing, since it is
  what makes the AC3 cite correction (below) meaningful: the `:1297`-era cite
  sits in the doc of the row directly above, so a new doc citing the
  derivation correctly had to land beside it rather than pointing at the
  wrong spot on its own.
- **Mirrors #1312's idiom exactly**: inline `finGatherInputs` (unseeded
  buffer), a goroutine running `finGatherReadings`, a 500ms sleep past two
  200ms poll ticks, one `probeSyncBuffer.Write`, `<-done`. No second tick
  constant.
- **Two builds off one held outcome argument** (`trailOutcomeNoRowMatched`,
  identical across both, asserted on neither directly — only its agreement
  across the two records is checked):
  - `first := finTrailerBuild(outcome, sighting)` — the measured record, built
    from the carrier the classifying poll actually filled.
  - `second := finTrailerBuild(outcome, finTrailerSighting(trailScan(stdout.Bytes()), 0, secondBound))`
    — the contrast, where `secondBound := trailWaitForTrailer(&stdout, finGatherTrailerWait).BoundFrom`
    is read directly off the call. **No `trailObservation` is ever bound** —
    mirroring #1312's row and its file-header rationale (`.Line` and the
    decoded pointer are the two things the carrier exists to keep out of
    reach, and with nothing in scope a later edit cannot `%v` one into a
    failure). **No `finSightingFrom` constructor** was added — the shipped
    `finTrailerSighting(scan, staleness, boundFrom)` is the only route to a
    carrier from a scan, and it's how every pre-existing call site reaches
    the builder.
  - The second carrier's `Staleness` is handed `0`, deliberately: the row
    asserts nothing about it, and the two ways to fill it for real (bind an
    observation, forbidden above; or a third `trailWaitForTrailer` call) each
    build a carrier whose duration and discriminator come from two different
    observations — the exact defect shape this family exists to prevent. The
    doc states the consequence plainly: this row does not discriminate a
    builder deriving `Bounded` from `Staleness != 0` — that mutant dies at
    the record tier (`TestFinTrailerRecordCarriesTheBoundAndItsDiscriminator`'s
    250ms start-bound and aborted rows).
- **Seven assertions**: premise (`first.State == trailSeen`, `Fatalf`), the
  measured bound (`first.BoundFrom == trailBoundFromMiss`, `Fatalf`), the
  published boolean (`first.Bounded`, `Errorf`), the contrast bound and
  boolean (`second.BoundFrom == trailBoundFromStart`, `!second.Bounded`,
  both `Errorf`), a shared-field table (`Outcome`/`State`/`Subtype`/
  `IsError`/`TerminalReason`/`StopReason` — the load-bearing half of AC2,
  since without it the two records could differ for any reason at all), and
  a `Detail`-content check (`strings.Contains` against each bound constant —
  new, since the record-tier test only asserts `Detail != ""`).
- **AC3, three prose corrections**, all in `finding_run_gather_test.go`
  before the insertion point:
  - `finGatherTrailerWait`'s wall-clock accounting now names both unseeded
    rows ("THESE TWO ARE THIS FILE'S ONLY WALL CLOCK") — the old "this
    file's only wall clock" clause would have gone false the moment this row
    landed.
  - `TestFinGatherSightingReportsTheMissBound`'s own doc ("why this row
    costs wall clock, and why no other one does") is re-stated to exempt the
    new row — found while sizing, not enumerated in the ticket body, but
    inside AC3's leading clause.
  - The two `Bounded`-derivation cites (previously `finding_trailer_evidence_test.go:209-215`,
    which #1320/#1325 had shifted onto unrelated outcome-membership prose)
    now point at `:247-253`, the actual `Bounded is BoundFrom ==
    trailBoundFromMiss AND NOTHING ELSE` derivation.
  - Two cross-file cites to `TestFinSightingReachesNoScanType` bumped
    (`:1696` → `:1916`) in `finding_trailer_evidence_test.go`, since the
    insertion moved that symbol down.

`TestFinGatherRecordPublishesTheMeasuredMissBound` is the only new test.

the Detail check; `finTrailerSighting` dropping `StopReason` → RED at the
shared-field table.

## Patterns established

- **Separating two records by measurement rather than by argument requires
  holding every other input identical across both builds** — same outcome
  value, same bytes, same builder — so a shared-field table becomes the
  load-bearing half of the contrast: without it, a difference between the
  two records could be attributed to anything.
- **A carrier field the row asserts nothing about should be handed the
  no-measurement zero value rather than measured for real**, when the only
  ways to measure it for real either re-bind a forbidden observation type or
  assemble a carrier from two distinct observations (the same defect shape
  the carrier type exists to prevent). Document the abstention and name
  which existing row actually kills the mutant it would otherwise leave
  undiscriminated.

## Lessons learned

(From the PR body and code review; see PR #1334.)

- **An in-file line-count-changing edit stales cites *into* that file, not
  just the cites the insertion itself shifts — and the blast radius follows
  the `^@@` diff hunks, not the ticket's own enumeration.** First review
  round (recorded in memory as `architect-1316-an-insertion-invalidates-line-cites-below-it`)
  found the AC3 prose edit alone grew a comment from 14 to 16 lines,
  shifting everything below `:151` by +2 — ten cites went stale behind it,
  none of them the two the spec's own § Cite maintenance enumerated. Rework
  swept both cite shapes (bare `(:NNN)` self-cites and cross-file
  `file.go:NNN` cites) and verified every referent against `git show main:`
  before and after, finding three more the table-anchored sweep missed: one
  whose cite sat >30 chars into its line, one that continues onto the next
  physical line so no single-line pattern sees it whole, and one that
  happened to match its referent's text verbatim by coincidence. Also
  attributed two pre-existing wrong cites (gaps of ~124 and hundreds of
  lines, too large to be this PR's +2 shift) as out of scope rather than
  folding them in — recorded separately as
  `code-review-attribute-a-stale-cite-by-gap-size`.
- **A universal claim ("every X call site … does Y") stated in the doc of
  the row that is about to add a counterexample to X is false the moment
  that row's own code lands below it.** The first review round's SHOULD FIX:
  "every `finTrailerBuild` call site in this package" was falsified by the
  new row's own two call sites 120 lines below the claim. Fixed by scoping
  to "before this row" — a length-identical substitution, so nothing else
  in the file had to re-shift.
- **`go test -overlay` grades a mutant per-assertion without touching the
  worktree**, which is what turns "seven branches" from a count into
  evidence — two of the four mandated mutations proved assertions that
  wouldn't have been visible from an all-green run.
