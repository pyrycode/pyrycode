# Ticket #1385 — Map claude's `system/thinking_tokens` subtype to a rate-bounded daemon event

Fourth arm on the `emitSystemSubtype` dispatch #1380 built, after #1382 (`task_updated`) and #1381
(`background_tasks_changed`). `thinking_tokens` is claude's only mid-turn proof of life on the
stream-json surface — during a long assistant turn nothing else crosses stdout, so a client showing
"thinking" cannot separate a slow answer from a wedge. What separates this ticket from its three
siblings: the payload is two integers rather than capped strings, the mapping is **rate-bounded** rather
than 1:1, and the parser gains its **first piece of cross-line state** to make that possible.

## Implementation

- **`turnevent.ThinkingProgress`** (`internal/turnevent/event.go`) — new variant, two fields:
  `EstimatedTokens` (claude's cumulative estimate, **restarts near zero at every inference-request
  boundary within one turn** — not monotonic, not a turn total) and `EstimatedTokensDelta` (the emitting
  line's own per-line increment — received deltas do not sum to the turn's total, since the rate bound
  drops most lines: 674 tokens of delta arrive as 243 across 8 events on the committed capture). No
  `TruncatedFields`: two `int`s cannot grow, so a permanently-nil cut report would claim a bound that
  doesn't exist. Same two deliberate drops as its siblings (`session_id`, `uuid`), absent from the decode
  target itself.
- **The rule** (`Parser.emitThinkingProgress`, `internal/streamsup/parser.go`) accumulates
  `estimated_tokens_delta` into a new unexported field `thinkingSinceEmit`, emits when the accumulated
  delta crosses `minThinkingTokensPerEvent = 64`, and resets to 0 on `consumeLine`'s `result` arm
  (unconditional, both result subtypes). Guard: `d <= 0` lines are consumed with no accumulation.
- **The crossing test is written subtracted (`d >= bound - acc`), not additive (`acc += d; if acc >=
  bound`).** The two are arithmetic identical for every representable value and the additive form
  overflows: `estimated_tokens_delta: math.MaxInt64` decodes cleanly into `int`, and on a nonzero
  residual `acc + d` wraps to roughly `-2^63` — the comparison then reads false and the turn's liveness
  signal is silently dead until the next `result`. Subtracted, both operands of `bound - acc` are in
  `[1, bound]` by the invariant `acc ∈ [0, bound-1]`, so the failure is unrepresentable rather than
  guarded against. Flagged as a MUST FIX in architect self-review before commit (the first spec draft
  wrote the additive form); closed before the PR opened.
- **`minThinkingTokensPerEvent = 64`** — a `min`, not a `max` (smallest delta that earns an event, so the
  file's `max*` naming would read backwards), and the package's first constant bounding **frequency**
  rather than size: two `int`s cannot blow an envelope, so there is no size arithmetic to make. The
  ceiling is measured: the smallest of the capture's four bursts totals 126 tokens of delta, and any
  bound above that lets a whole burst go silent (at 127, burst 3 emits nothing while the others still
  do). 64 is roughly half that ceiling — a future burst half the size of the smallest one observed still
  emits from a zero residual.
- **No-silent-burst holds by construction, not tuning.** The accumulator only ever grows within a turn,
  so a rule keyed on the per-line *delta* is immune to claude's cumulative counter restarting at each
  inference request — unlike a high-water-mark rule over the cumulative counter, which the capture's own
  burst 2 (tops out at 167, below burst 1's 184) proves goes silent for a whole burst.
- **Parser gains one field**, `thinkingSinceEmit int`, no lock: the single-writer invariant
  (`os/exec` drives `Write` from exactly one goroutine) already covered this, but it's the first field
  for which that invariant does real work rather than describing the partial-line buffer. `Parser`'s doc
  ("turn-stateless") is amended in place rather than reworded elsewhere — it now names the one
  accumulator, its single `result` boundary, and states it is a token *counter*, never a memory of
  anything claude said.
- **Comment corrections landing in this PR**, all falsified the moment the arm landed: `consumeLine`'s
  drop comment (no longer lists `thinking_tokens` among unmapped subtypes), `ignoredLineTypes`'s
  `CORRECTED (#1380)` block (dropped its now-false "all three" count in favor of a pointer to
  `emitSystemSubtype`'s case arms), `Parser`'s type doc (turn-stateless claim), `emitBackgroundTaskRoster`'s
  doc (re-scoped its "parser holds no cross-line state" refusal-mechanism claim to "no roster, no
  per-task memory" — the refusal against synthesizing a finish event is unweakened), and
  `watchdog.go`'s comment citing the old absolute claim (one clause; the awaiting-flag half is
  unaffected and left alone).
- **Realclaude mirror** (`internal/e2e/realclaude/ptyrunner_byte_equivalence_test.go`, separate commit) —
  two consequences of adding `thinking_tokens` to `parserIgnoredTypes`' mapped set, only one of which the
  ticket body named:
  1. The fixture must cross the bound. The bare `{"type":"system","subtype":"thinking_tokens"}`
     convention (deliberate for the three unconditional-emit siblings) cannot hold for a rate-bounded
     mapping, which emits zero for a payload-free line by design. Replaced with a payload carrying
     `estimated_tokens`/`estimated_tokens_delta` both `1024` (16× the bound).
  2. The cross-runner **sequence** comparison (`shapeFilterDrops` → `TestPtyRunnerVsStreamRunner_
     StructuralEquivalence`, live, in `make preship`) would un-filter ~10 `thinking_tokens` lines/turn
     and compare them against zero on the ptyrunner side — reopening #1218's measured RED. Fixed with a
     fourth, narrow table, `expectedStreamRunnerOnlySubtypes`, consulted by `shapeFilterDrops` only —
     deliberately kept separate from `parserIgnoredTypes` ("does the parser map this?") and from
     `expectedStreamRunnerOnly` ("would widening this pre-authorise a genuine divergence in the SET
     check?"). New pin, `TestShapeFilterKeepsThinkingTokensOutOfTheSequence`, asserts the two tables
     deliberately disagree for this subtype.

## Patterns established

- **A rate-bounded mapping is a fourth shape in the `emitSystemSubtype` family**, distinct from
  #1380/#1381/#1382's pure-function-of-one-line mappings. It needs its own cross-line-state doc
  amendments at the type and at the field, and it is the first constant in the file arguing frequency
  instead of size — a future rate-bounded subtype should reuse the "no envelope arithmetic, here's why"
  shape rather than mimicking the `max*` cap comments.
- **Write a crossing/threshold test subtracted, not additively, whenever the accumulated quantity is
  untrusted input.** The subtracted form makes overflow unrepresentable instead of guarded against — a
  reusable shape for any future accumulate-and-emit rule over claude-derived numbers.
- **A mapped-set entry in the realclaude mirror has a second consequence beyond the fixture**: it
  un-filters from the cross-runner sequence comparison, not just the presence/absence check. A future
  subtype added to the mapped set should check `shapeFilterDrops`' four tables, not just
  `parserIgnoredTypes`.
- **`parserIgnoredTypes` ("does the parser map this?") and one-sidedness ("do both runners emit this?")
  can agree by accident** and come apart the moment either changes — conflating them into one table
  forces a false statement into one or the other.

## Lessons learned

(From the PR body — developer's notes, lightly trimmed.)

- Adding a mapped-set entry to the realclaude mirror has a second consequence the ticket body didn't
  name: it un-filters the subtype from the cross-runner *sequence* comparison, which would have compared
  ~10 streamrunner shapes against zero on the ptyrunner side and reopened #1218's RED on the live
  pre-ship gate — a real-claude run's worth of wall clock to discover if left for later. Confirmed by
  observation (removing the fourth table reproduces the failure), not predicted.
- The spec's `674 → 232` post-bound token total was an arithmetic slip; the measured, shipped value is
  243. Event count (8) and emit indices matched the spec exactly, so the *rule* was right — worth
  re-measuring a spec's own numbers before shipping a doc that repeats them.
- Bound-derived vs. literal test fixtures is a per-assertion decision, not a file-wide rule: the count
  pin (AC3) must be a literal or the constant sits on both sides of the assertion and survives mutation,
  but the overflow and turn-reset tests assert properties that hold at *every* bound value, so hardcoding
  a bound-adjacent number there made them collateral damage of any future bound change. Deriving those
  setups from the constant keeps a legitimate bound change re-deriving exactly the one number that should
  move.
- `go test -overlay` mutation testing (no worktree writes) is what turned "8 of 9 mutants caught" from a
  claim into evidence, and is what surfaced the one gap code review later confirmed independently.

## Code review notes (PASS, one SHOULD FIX unaddressed — below threshold to block)

- **SHOULD FIX, not fixed in this PR.** The `d <= 0` guard (`parser.go:973`) is load-bearing — removing
  it leaves the *entire* package green, including the whole offline `e2e_realclaude` set, because the
  existing "delta negative" test row only checks that a single negative-delta line itself emits nothing,
  which holds either way. What the guard actually prevents is every *subsequent* line going silent for
  the rest of the turn (reproduced by the reviewer via overlay: guard present → 5 events over 5
  full-quantum lines following one negative line; guard removed → 0). The shipped behaviour is correct;
  this is a test-strength gap in `TestParser_ThinkingProgressSilentWithoutPayload`, two lines to close.
  Not merge-blocking — one SHOULD FIX is under the three-finding rework threshold.
- **NIT, not fixed.** `watchdog.go`'s amended comment leaves a dangling one-word line (`// Unlike`) from
  the reflow; `gofmt` doesn't rewrap comments so it survives.
- **NIT, informational — not a defect.** The overflow test's doc comment claims the additive form "also
  emits on the extreme line itself," which is measured false for the `from_a_nonzero_residual` subtest
  (the additive form's wraparound swallows the extreme line too, emitting 0 not 1). True only of the
  `from_a_zero_accumulator` subtest. The test's actual assertion (the *second* event is the discriminating
  one) is unaffected and in fact stronger than the comment claims.
- Mutation testing: 8 of 9 mutants caught RED at the intended row (bound constant, crossing-test form,
  reset removal, `>=`→`>`, field zeroing, field swap, bare fixture, fourth-table removal); the 9th (guard
  removal) is the SHOULD FIX above.
- Blast radius double-checked: six `turnevent.Event` switch sites touch this variant, not the four the
  spec's downstream table names — `cmd/pyry/acp_turn_stream.go` and `cmd/pyry/interactive_turn_v2.go`
  were confirmed benign (both fall through a `default`/no-`default` path identical to the three
  background-task variants' existing behaviour).

## Out of scope (deliberately deferred)

- The mobile v2 wire (#1386, blocked by this ticket) and the desktop ACP surface (#1262) — no protocol
  type, payload struct, or route added here; `turnbridge.MapEvent`'s `default:` arm drops the variant at
  the daemon boundary exactly as it does for the three background-task siblings today.
- The `d <= 0` guard test gap (code review SHOULD FIX, above) — deferred, not required for merge.
- `idleStall`'s stale 240s-threshold rationale (`watchdog.go`) — the capture refutes it (33 lines
  interleaved mid-turn on this exact surface), but it belongs to its own ticket per the architect spec;
  not touched here.
- A per-turn wall-clock ceiling on event rate, and deriving the delta from the cumulative counter as a
  hedge against a future claude dropping `estimated_tokens_delta` — both open questions in the spec,
  neither needed by anything this ticket ships.
