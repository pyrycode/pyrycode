# Ticket #1326 — Retire #1286's trailer plant from the artifact writer's channel list

One file, `internal/e2e/realclaude/finding_artifact_write_test.go` (177
insertions / 58 deletions), prose surgery plus one relocation and one
deletion of a logically redundant check. No signature, type or constant
changed; no new sweep; no repad; offline, `e2e_realclaude` build tag. Split
from #1323; blocked on #1325 (merged) and #1324 (merged).

## Context

Since #1320, `finTrailerBuild` takes the gather's `finSighting` carrier (no
`.Line`, no `*resultTrailer`) in place of a `trailObservation`. The needle
`finWriteInputs` planted in the trailer scan's `Line` was therefore consumed
at **fixture-construction time**: `finTrailerSighting` copies four scalars
off the decode and never reads `scan.Line`, so it never enters
`finRecordInputs`. #1286's Plant #3 and its three channel-3 preconditions
certified a reduction the writer under test does not perform — a channel
running one tier below the instrument it was guarding. #1325 shipped the
identical retirement one tier down in `finding_trailer_evidence_test.go` and
was this ticket's template.

The trap #1323's body had conflated: the plant's surface and the fixture's
surface are different sets. Retiring the *claim* is right; deleting the
*fixture* (`finWriteTrailerPad`, `finWritePlantedTrailerScan`) is not — both
keep live consumers.

## Implementation

- **The retired claim is stated once, not six times.** A single `RETIRED BY
  #1326` note replaces the "Why the trailer plant lands INSIDE the cap"
  section on `TestFinWriteArtifactsCarryNoCapturedBytes`'s doc comment, in
  #1325's shipped shape: what died, why it was a test of nothing at this
  tier, where the claim now holds by symbol
  (`TestFinGatherReturnsNoCapturedBytes`, `finding_run_gather_test.go`,
  which sweeps the `finSighting` carrier as its third return and asserts the
  same in-cap precondition in code), and what survives. Every other site —
  the marked call site in `finWriteInputs`, the plant list, both header
  claims, the sweep's failure-message enumeration — carries a short,
  locally-true statement and points at that one note rather than re-arguing
  it. The dangling `#1321's to revisit` deferral at the call site is
  discharged, not forwarded: no site names a future ticket as the owner of
  remaining work.
- **Plant list renumbered, not left with a gap.** Entry 3 ("the trailer
  scan's Line") is deleted; the surviving two entries stay 1–2, and the
  `channel 4 (reap stderr)` `Fatalf` prefixes become `channel 3` — three
  occurrences in `TestFinWriteArtifactsCarryNoCapturedBytes`, including the
  one carrying the `trailAdmitProof` premise that is the *only* thing making
  that plant non-vacuous. The old→new mapping (channel 3 now means the reap
  stderr; a "Plant #3" reference in history still means the trailer) is
  recorded in the retirement note itself.
- **One guard relocated, one check deleted as redundant.** The pre-build
  clean check on the embedded trailer sub-record is the one surviving
  assertion that rests on the in-cap plant — `resultTrailer` has no
  `result` member, so the four decoded scalars cannot carry the needle at
  any pad, and without the guard the row degenerates into asserting a shape
  the type already forbids. The guard (`scan.State != trailSeen` as a
  labelled *diagnosis* Fatalf, then `!strings.Contains(scan.Line,
  trailNeedle)` as the real guard, both `Fatalf` and before the build) moves
  onto that check. The old needle-offset-vs-cap arithmetic check is deleted
  outright rather than relocated: `trailScan` records `Line:
  reachCapCommand(...)`, and `reachCapCommand` returns the line unchanged at
  ≤512 bytes and `line[:512]+marker` above it, so `strings.Contains(scan.Line,
  trailNeedle)` is true **iff** the offset check would have passed — the two
  predicates are the same fact computed two ways. Keeping the one that reads
  the retained copy over the one that re-derives the capping rule means a
  future change to `reachCapCommand` (e.g. rune-boundary trimming) can't
  turn the offset arithmetic into a stale model of the cap while the
  containment check keeps reading the truth.
- **Two survivors keep running, each re-arguing its own survival.** The
  pre-build clean check's comment now names the right sub-builder
  (`finTrailerSighting`, not `finTrailerBuild`) and states its register as
  *prospective* — nothing carries the needle into this sub-record today; the
  guard is against a future reader of the line. The verbatim-output pairing
  subtest (`TestFinWriteArtifactPublishesNoVerbatimModelOutput`) survives on
  a **weaker, cap-independent property**: `trailPaddedTrailer` splices
  `trailNeedle` into its `result` field at every pad, so the wire line the
  four published scalars sat beside carries the needle regardless of the
  cap — it needs no in-cap guard of its own and does not acquire one.
- **`finWriteTrailerPad` and `finWritePlantedTrailerScan` are kept, docs
  re-stated to the position they now hold.** The pad stays 0 and the
  fixture stays `trailPaddedTrailer` (not swapped to a needle-free fixture —
  `trailFixtureTrailer` renders `is_error` false, which would turn a quarter
  of the pairing subtest's contrast into theatre). Offsets re-measured at
  `a270bac` rather than carried forward from `4bc5f5b`: pad 0 → 385 bytes,
  needle at 104–146; pad 200 → 585 bytes, needle in-cap but `terminal_reason`
  cut; needle crosses the cap at pad 367. The pad doc also records a second
  reason not to touch the constant: `finGatherOverCapPad`'s doc

- **Two stale cross-file cites corrected, both by symbol.** The

## Patterns established

- **When a retired claim is argued in six places, state the full argument
  once and let the other five point at it by symbol.** Six independent
  copies is the drift surface this family (see #1325's own "Patterns
  established") keeps paying to fix; a single `RETIRED BY #N` note plus
  short local pointers is now the shipped shape for this kind of
  cross-tier claim retirement, twice in two tickets.
- **A "logically identical" pair of checks is worth collapsing only after
  proving the equivalence from the production code the checks are about**,
  not from reading the two assertions side by side. Here the proof was
  `trailScan`'s `Line: reachCapCommand(...)` plus `reachCapCommand`'s own
  two-armed return — establishing `strings.Contains(...)` and the offset
  arithmetic are the same predicate, not just usually-agreeing ones. Prefer
  the check that reads the artefact over the one that re-derives the rule,
  because only the former stays true automatically if the rule changes.
- **A guard that used to certify a live plant, once retired, must be
  re-labelled in its own prospective register** rather than inheriting
  present-tense language ("would be caught here") that is false today. The
  pre-build clean check's comment change is the concrete instance; #1325's
  key-scan comment is the shipped precedent for the register.

## Lessons learned

- The channel-renumbering edit (`channel 4` → `channel 3`) touches three
  `Fatalf` call sites in the same function, one of which carries the only
  precondition making the reap-stderr plant non-vacuous
  (`trailAdmitProof`). A renumbering pass done as a bulk find-replace over
  the block risks dropping that premise silently while staying green (it
  would just make the test build a fan-out with the assertion missing, not
  fail to compile). Verify count and content of surviving preconditions
  after any renumbering, not just that the test still passes.
- The two cross-file cites this ticket corrects (`finding_trailer_evidence_test.go`)
  were not just stale by line number — one target had been **deleted
  outright** and one had been **reversed** by #1325's rewrite. "Grep for the
  cited symbol and see if it still exists" is not sufficient; a hit that
  exists can still argue the opposite of what the citing prose needs.
- The mandated mutation (repad `finWriteTrailerPad` past the measured
  threshold of 367, confirm the relocated guard fails loudly, then confirm
  `TestFinWriteArtifactPublishesNoVerbatimModelOutput` stays green because
  its pairing rests on the wire line rather than the retained copy) is the
  observed evidence for this ticket's central claim — that the two
  survivors rest on genuinely different properties (in-cap retained copy vs.
  cap-independent wire line) rather than one silently depending on the
  other.
