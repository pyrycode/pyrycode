# Ticket #1462 — Probe instrument: `finGatherReadings` carries the sighting route's certified ordering from its caller (offline)

`trailClassifyRun`'s `trailGateAbsentOwesNone` arm consults the pinned-pid
sighting route on a step-1 guard that reads `readings.Ordering.Value` alone:
unfilled, it answers `trailOutcomeVoidSightingRouteNotStaged` before the
route is ever consulted. #1458 staged the route's other input — the pinned
pid — and no outcome moved, precisely because the guard is single-sided.
This ticket ships the half that unblocks it: `finGatherInputs` gains an
`Ordering trailOrderResult` field, appended after `PinnedPid`, and
`finGatherReadings` carries it **whole** onto `trailRunReadings.Ordering`.
Driven offline through the shipped gather and classifier, a certified
ordering now reaches `trailOutcomeAliveAtSightingByOrdering` and
`trailOutcomeVoidPinnedPidDidNotEstablish` — two outcomes reachable before
only from hand-built classifier-tier fixtures. **No live caller is wired**,
and `finExitRunProbe` is untouched by design: the field's own doc records
why one cannot be added here, and that the resolving ticket does not exist
yet. Offline throughout: no live claude, no credentials.

PR [#1464](https://github.com/pyrycode/pyrycode/pull/1464). Spec:
[`docs/specs/architecture/1462-gather-carries-the-certified-ordering.md`](../../specs/architecture/1462-gather-carries-the-certified-ordering.md)
(architect self-review PASS). One code-review round, PASS with two
non-blocking NITs.

## Implementation

- **`finGatherInputs` gains its ninth field, `Ordering trailOrderResult`**,
  appended at the end after `PinnedPid` — never mid-struct, which is what
  keeps the file's in-body bare `:NNN` citation tail from displacing (the tax
  #1452's insertion paid in full, and #1458 paid again in code review). Its
  doc carries four claims in the shape its neighbours already use: the one
  admissible producer is `trailCertifyOrdering`'s own output, never a
  `trailOrderResult{Value: trailOrderCertified}` literal (which would let
  everything downstream pass against an ordering predicate that certifies
  nothing — the bypass `trailSightingPremisesFor` closed on the fixture
  side); it travels **whole**, never narrowed to `.Value`, because
  `trailEstablishSighting` takes the record whole and says so in its own
  first arm ("passed through WHOLE and never re-decided here"); the zero
  `trailOrderResult` (`Value ""`) degrades to the named
  `trailOutcomeVoidSightingRouteNotStaged` reading rather than to a claim,
  which is why no C10 contract check is owed over it; and no live caller
  fills it, with none permitted here — see below. A fifth paragraph states
  the captured-bytes posture: `trailCertifyOrdering` takes three `bool`s, so
  no argv-shaped or `ps`-shaped value can reach a `trailOrderResult` through
  its admissible route at all — a structural reduction, not a disciplinary
  one, so the field carries no "NEVER a raw ps column" prohibition of the
  kind `RunnerPath`/`PinnedPid` do.
- **The carriage, in `finGatherReadings`:** `readings.Ordering = in.Ordering`
  — a bare whole-value assignment beside the existing
  `PyryExited`/`ClaudeState`/`PinnedPid` tail, no default, no normalisation,
  no `trailIsOrderValue` call. A new paragraph above the tail explains why
  the gather does **not** certify the ordering itself even though it looks
  possible: it already holds two of `trailCertifyOrdering`'s three premises
  (`in.PyryExited`, and its own trailer sighting), but `holdHeld` is a fact
  about a FIFO the *caller* holds, and a certification computed from two
  premises and a guess is worse than none — the same argument the function
  already makes about its own key-name cap.
- **Why no live caller can be wired here, structurally rather than by
  scoping choice:** `trailCertifyOrdering` needs `trailerSighted`, and the
  trailer is sighted by `trailWaitForTrailer`, whose two non-test call sites
  (`finGatherReadings` itself, `trailRigGather`) both report the sighting
  *after* the call a certification would need to be passed to. Recovering it
  with an earlier `trailWaitForTrailer` at the call site degrades the
  gather's own sighting to `trailBoundFromStart` — a discriminator whose own
  doc says it BOUNDS NOTHING — and that degraded observation is the one that
  fills the published `BoundFrom`, `Staleness` and the gate. Resolving that
  circularity is design work for a ticket that does not exist yet;
  `finExitRunProbe` is left untouched and #1458's precedent (wire the probe
  at step 3b) is named explicitly as the wrong shape to follow here.
- **`TestFinGatherStagedOrderingReachesTheSightingOutcomes`**, a new
  three-row table reusing `TestFinGatherHalfStagedRouteMovesNoOutcome`'s
  recipe (seed `trailKeyNamesNoTerminalReason()` under a `RunnerPath` that
  reduces to streamrunner — without both halves the gate lands elsewhere and
  every claim is about a different arm). The ordering comes from
  `trailCertifyOrdering(true, true, true)`, never a literal; the pin rows
  carry the real shapes `pinClassifyState` fills on each arm (running:
  `PPID`+live `StateColumn`; awaiting-reap: both filled before the zombie
  check, `ExitStatus 0`; gone: non-zero `ExitStatus`, neither `PPID` nor
  `StateColumn`, since that arm returns before a row is parsed) rather than
  `trailSightingPin`, which `finGatherInputs.PinnedPid`'s own doc rules out
  by name at this tier (it sets no `StateColumn`/`ToolStderr`, the shape a
  live *failing* read produces). Three premises (`Fatalf`) turn a vacuous
  comparison into a stop: the gate reads `trailGateAbsentOwesNone`, and
  `Ordering`/`PinnedPid` cross whole (`==`, never `.Value`/`.Verdict` alone).
  Claims are taken over the readings **the gather produced**, never a
  typed-in outcome: `trailClassifyRun(readings).Value`, `.Route ==
  trailRouteSighting`, `.RouteReason`, and a non-vacuity cross-check —
  the row's declared reason must equal `trailEstablishSighting(...).Reason`
  over the gather's own readings, so a row can't agree with a classifier
  that hardcoded a reason the predicate no longer emits. No needle plant, no
  captured-bytes sweep (that's #1463's, on #1452's "a route gets a sibling
  test" precedent), no `RowsScanned`/`MatchCount` comparison (step 1 answers
  before steps 3–8 move either count, and this package's parallelism makes
  cross-row count comparisons flaky by construction), no `trailAdmitProof`
  row (C4/C5 sit above step 1 and would re-prove the contract block instead
  of the arm).
- **`TestFinGatherHalfStagedRouteMovesNoOutcome` (AC 3) — rows unchanged,
  doc revised.** The `Ordering` field used to be unfilled *structurally* (no
  field existed, so `readings.Ordering.Value == ""` was a tautology); it is
  now unfilled *by choice*, and the test's premise becomes a genuine check
  that the rows really are half-staged rather than a restated compile-time
  fact. The claim itself — a caller filling one input without the other must
  not defeat the single-sided guard — is unchanged.
- **AC 4's comment sweep — ten sites across two files, re-measured rather
  than inherited**, grepping the discriminating substring `gather` in both
  `trail_run_outcome_test.go` and `finding_run_gather_test.go`. Two claims
  travel welded in most sites and were split rather than flipped together:
  "no gather stages the ordering" goes flatly false (a field now exists);
  "the not-staged answer is what every live run produces" stays true (no
  live caller fills it) and must not be "corrected" into a claim that live
  runs now reach the finding. The load-bearing site is the no-C10 note
  inside `trailClassifyRun`: its supporting sentence goes false, but the
  conclusion stands on the note's *next* paragraph — the ordering side is
  now decided by the step-1 guard rather than by a contract check, "a
  READING and not a contract violation," the same move the note already made
  on the pin side for #1458. No C10 was added. All **five** stale `#1457`
  attributions (#1457 is CLOSED, the parent this ticket split from) are
  repaired to name the carriage as landed and the staging as still unowned —
  never repointed to "#1462 stages it," which AC 4 explicitly forbids, and
  never a placeholder number, since no wiring ticket exists yet. Two sites
  were verified as correctly **not** edited: the two-tier plant note (past
  tense, about the basis #1458 moved) and `trailCertifyOrdering`'s "premises
  are supplied, not recovered" doc (about the `holdHeld` premise, which this
  change makes *more* true, not less).
- **AC 5's numeral-and-ordinal doc-block sweep (six edits, one block).**
  Three sites go four→five (the staged-set count, in cardinal and ordinal
  form — `\bfour\b` alone misses "fourth"). The four zero-polarity bullets
  gain a fifth, arguing the new field's zero the same way the other four
  argue theirs. The opening enumeration's `#1281's four values` stays
  correctly untouched — a historical attribution, still true — while its
  *list* gains a ninth clause in the same `#NNNN routed in from …` form its
  last two clauses use, saying *routed in*, never *stages* (the exact
  correction AC 4 forbids, and the site a developer is likeliest to write it
  at by reflex). The eight-argument positional shorthand (prose count and
  the illustrative call) both move to nine, in field order.
- **Mutation evidence, reproduced independently in code review** via `go
  test -overlay` (no worktree writes): narrowing the carriage to
  `trailOrderResult{Value: in.Ordering.Value}` reddens the new test's
  whole-value premise on the `Detail` diff, not a `.Value`-only comparison
  in disguise; widening the step-1 guard to `&&` reddens
  `TestFinGatherHalfStagedRouteMovesNoOutcome` onto
  `run-void-path-owes-no-reason` under `sighting-reason-ordering-uncertified`
  — the exact defeat #1448's guard doc argues about; swapping the two
  refutation rows' expected reasons reddens both the published-reason
  assertion and the predicate cross-check, proving neither row is dead
  weight.

## Patterns established

- **A field whose producer is structurally incapable of carrying captured
  bytes needs no producer-side prohibition, only a statement of the
  structural fact.** `trailCertifyOrdering`'s three-`bool` signature is why
  `Ordering` carries no "NEVER a raw ps column" clause the way `RunnerPath`
  and `PinnedPid` do — the reduction is in the type, not in a rule a
  developer must remember. Confirms [`codebase/1439.md`](1439.md)'s
  narrow-the-parameter argument, now read from the consumer's side.
- **Two welded claims travelling in one sentence must be split at the site,
  not resolved by picking the true one and deleting the other.** "No gather
  stages the ordering" and "every live run reaches the not-staged answer"
  shared ten sentences across two files; one went false and one stayed true,
  and the fix at each site was separating them, never silently dropping the
  half that's now false or "upgrading" the half that stayed true into a
  stronger claim it doesn't support.
- **A stale ticket attribution and a still-true claim can share one clause,
  and repairing the attribution is not the same operation as repointing the
  number.** Five sites named a closed parent ticket (#1457) as the one that
  would stage the ordering. The correct repair names the carriage as landed
  and the staging as unowned; renaming the number to the ticket that shipped
  the carriage would assert something false (that ticket doesn't stage it
  either), and inventing a placeholder for the not-yet-filed wiring ticket
  would be worse than naming no number at all.
- **A test whose premise was a compile-time tautology becomes a real check
  the moment the field it reads exists, and the test's own doc must say
  which of the two it now is.** `TestFinGatherHalfStagedRouteMovesNoOutcome`
  didn't change its rows or its assertions — only what those assertions mean
  changed, and that shift needed to be written down so a later reader
  doesn't mistake a newly-behavioural check for a restated impossibility.

## Lessons learned

**From the PR body's own "Lessons learned" section** (developer):

- **A count-literal site inside one field's own bullet does not resolve the
  same way as the block's other count sites.** AC 5 listed `"over a fourth
  field"` among the sites that "go to five," but that clause sits inside
  `PinnedPid`'s own bullet and says *its* argued home carried the closing
  sentence over a fourth field — which stays true, since `PinnedPid` is
  still the fourth field regardless of how many more are added later.
  Rewriting it to "fifth" would have made `PinnedPid`'s bullet assert
  something false about a field it is not. The fix: add a cross-reference
  (`— the ordering below carries it over a fifth —`) rather than mutate the
  ordinal in place, so both numbers stay present and correct and an ordinal
  sweep still surfaces the site.
- **A stale attribution and a still-true claim sharing one sentence is not
  fixed by repointing the number.** Five sites said "#1457 has not yet
  staged the ordering" — one clause naming a now-closed ticket welded to a
  claim (the ordering isn't staged) that survives this change unchanged. The
  fix is to split the two clauses apart, not to swap `#1457` for `#1462`:
  "#1462 stages it" would have been false and is the one correction AC 4
  explicitly forbids.
